# Life Areas

A starting framework for the `/review` skill's life check-in. These areas are a lens, not a cage — adjust to fit your reality.

## Areas

### Career
Work, professional growth, income, job satisfaction, skill development in your field.

**Indicators:** shipping work you're proud of, learning on the job, compensation trajectory, work-life boundaries.

### Health
Physical health, mental health, energy levels, sleep quality, exercise habits.

**Indicators:** consistent energy, manageable stress, regular movement, sleep that feels restorative.

### Home & Infra
Living space, homelab, home automation, systems and tools that support daily life.

**Indicators:** space feels functional, systems run without constant firefighting, infra serves you rather than consuming you.

### Hobbies
Creative projects, hands-on making (3D printing, woodworking, etc.), side projects done for fun, not obligation.

**Indicators:** regular time spent on something purely enjoyable, finishing things occasionally, not just starting.

### Relationships
Family, friends, community, social connection, quality time.

**Indicators:** feeling connected, regular contact with people who matter, not isolated.

### Learning
Skills you're building outside of work, curiosity-driven exploration, courses, books, experiments.

**Indicators:** actively learning something, curiosity engaged, growing in a direction that interests you.

## Rating Scale

| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 1 | Needs real attention — actively suffering or fully neglected |
| 2 | Below where you'd like — noticeable drag |
| 3 | Okay — not great, not bad, coasting |
| 4 | Good — getting attention, moving in the right direction |
| 5 | Thriving — this area feels solid right now |

## Notes

- These areas are a **starting framework**, not a rigid system. If an area doesn't fit, rename or replace it.
- Not every area needs to be a 5. The point is awareness, not perfection.
- Areas rated 1-2 are candidates for theme attention in `/review`, not emergencies to panic about.
- Some areas naturally trade off — pushing Career hard may lower Hobbies, and that's fine if it's intentional.
