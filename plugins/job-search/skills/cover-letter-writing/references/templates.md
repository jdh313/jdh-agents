# Cover Letter Templates

Templates organized by role type and company culture.

## Template 1: Technical Role at Startup

Best for: Engineering roles at fast-moving startups

```
Dear [Hiring Team / Name],

When I saw [Company]'s approach to [specific technical challenge], I immediately recognized the kind of problems I love solving. Your [specific product/feature] tackles [problem] in a way that aligns with my experience [building/optimizing] [related work].

At [Current/Recent Company], I [achievement 1 with metrics]. This required [technical skills] and taught me [relevant learning]. More recently, I [achievement 2 with metrics], which directly relates to your need for [their stated requirement].

What excites me most about [Company] is [specific aspect — mission, tech stack, team, growth stage]. I'm particularly drawn to [specific detail from research] and would love to contribute to [their goal].

I'd welcome the chance to discuss how my experience with [key skill] could help [Company] [achieve specific outcome]. Thank you for considering my application.

Best,
[Name]
```

**Tone**: Enthusiastic, specific, shows technical depth
**Length**: ~250 words

## Template 2: Senior Role at Enterprise

Best for: Senior/Staff roles at established companies

```
Dear [Mr./Ms. Last Name / Hiring Manager],

I am writing to express my interest in the [Position] role at [Company]. With [X] years of experience in [domain], including [key credential], I am confident in my ability to contribute to [Company]'s [specific initiative or team].

In my current role at [Company], I have [achievement 1 with metrics]. This experience has given me deep expertise in [relevant skill area]. Additionally, I [achievement 2], demonstrating my ability to [relevant capability they need].

[Company]'s commitment to [specific value/initiative] resonates with my professional philosophy. I am particularly interested in [specific aspect of role or team], where I believe my background in [relevant experience] would be valuable.

I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss how my experience aligns with [Company]'s needs. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
[Name]
```

**Tone**: Professional, measured, emphasizes reliability
**Length**: ~220 words

## Template 3: Mission-Driven Organization

Best for: Nonprofits, B-corps, mission-focused companies

```
Dear [Hiring Team],

[Company]'s mission to [their mission] speaks directly to why I became a [your profession]. The opportunity to apply my [technical skills] toward [their impact area] is exactly the kind of meaningful work I've been seeking.

At [Current Company], I [achievement that shows similar values or impact]. This experience taught me that technology is most powerful when it [connects to their mission]. I've also [achievement 2], demonstrating my commitment to [relevant value — efficiency, accessibility, sustainability, etc.].

Beyond technical skills, I bring [soft skill or value alignment]. I was particularly moved by [specific company initiative, blog post, or impact story], and I'm eager to contribute to [specific program or goal].

I would be honored to join [Company] in [their mission]. I look forward to the possibility of discussing how I can contribute to your important work.

Warmly,
[Name]
```

**Tone**: Values-driven, genuine, connects personal motivation to mission
**Length**: ~230 words

## Template 4: Career Transition

Best for: Moving between industries or role types

```
Dear [Hiring Team],

My path to [target field] may be unconventional, but my [X] years in [previous field] have equipped me with [transferable skills] that directly apply to [target role].

At [Previous Company], I [achievement that demonstrates transferable skill]. While the domain was [previous field], the core challenge — [universal challenge like optimization, user experience, data-driven decisions] — mirrors what [Company] faces in [their context].

I've been actively building [target field] skills through [courses, projects, certifications, side projects]. Most recently, I [relevant project or learning]. This preparation, combined with my foundation in [transferable skill], positions me to contribute meaningfully from day one.

What draws me to [Company] specifically is [reason]. I'm excited by the opportunity to bring [fresh perspective/cross-domain insight] to your team.

I would welcome the chance to discuss how my unique background could benefit [Company]. Thank you for considering my application.

Best regards,
[Name]
```

**Tone**: Confident but humble, emphasizes transferable value
**Length**: ~240 words

## Template 5: Referral Application

Best for: When you have an internal referral

```
Dear [Hiring Manager],

[Referrer Name] suggested I reach out about the [Position] opening. After learning about [Company]'s work on [specific project/initiative], I'm excited about the possibility of contributing to your team.

[Referrer] and I [how you know them], and they mentioned that [Company] is looking for someone with experience in [skill area]. At [Current Company], I [achievement directly relevant to their need]. I've also [second relevant achievement].

What particularly interests me about this role is [specific aspect]. [Referrer] shared [insight about team/culture/challenge], which aligns well with my experience [relevant background].

I would appreciate the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to [specific team or initiative]. Thank you for your consideration.

Best,
[Name]

P.S. [Referrer Name] can be reached at [contact] if you'd like to discuss my background with them.
```

**Tone**: Personal connection, specific, leverages referral appropriately
**Length**: ~200 words

## Customization Checklist

Before sending any cover letter:

- [ ] Company name correct and consistent throughout
- [ ] Role title matches job posting exactly
- [ ] Hiring manager name verified (if used)
- [ ] Achievements selected for relevance to THIS role
- [ ] Company-specific detail included (not generic)
- [ ] Tone matches company culture
- [ ] Length between 200-400 words
- [ ] No spelling or grammar errors
- [ ] Contact info matches resume
- [ ] Call to action included
